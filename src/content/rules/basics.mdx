# Basics

## Rolling the d20

There are four circumstances the d20 is rolled: ability checks, perception checks, saves, and attack rolls. Collectively, these are called checks.

### Saves

There are three kinds of Save: Reflex saves, Fortitude saves, and Will saves. Each adds a different ability modifier to the check:

| Save | Ability Modifier |
|---|---|
| Reflex | +DEX |
| Fortitude | +CON |
| Will | +WIS |

### Attack Rolls

When attacking, if you are using a weapon you have proficiency in or casting a spell you know, add your PB. By default, creatures are proficient in unarmed attacks.

### Advantage and Disadvantage

To make a check with Advantage, roll two d20s and take the higher result. 
To make a check with Disadvantage, roll two d20s and take the lower result.

Neither Advantage nor Disadvantage stack. If a check would be made with both Advantage and Disadvantage, the two cancel out and the roll is made normally.

### Proficiency and Expertise

Each character has a Proficiency Bonus (PB) based on their level. When making a check with Proficiency, add your character's PB to the check. Proficiency can only apply once per check.

| Level | PB |
|---|---|
| 1-4 | +2 |
| 5-8 | +3 |
| 9-12 | +4 |
| 13-16 | +5 |
| 17-20 | +6 |

Each character also has Expertise on checks made in certain circumstances. When making a check with Expertise, add your character's PB to the check. If the character would be adding their PB for another reason, this effectively doubles the PB on that check. Expertise can only apply once per check.

### Rerolls

When you are allowed to reroll a die, the reroll overwrites the previous roll, and the original result cannot be used. If the original roll had Advantage or Disadvantage, you only reroll whichever die rolled lowest.
