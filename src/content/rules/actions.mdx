# Actions

## General Principles

You can only perform one action at a time. In combat, this means you take one action per turn. In exploration or social situations, you must quit any ongoing action before choosing a new one.

## Bonus Actions

Some features give you access to a Bonus Action.

In combat, you can only perform a Bonus Action during your turn, and only once per turn, in addition to any timing restrictions the Bonus Action itself has.
