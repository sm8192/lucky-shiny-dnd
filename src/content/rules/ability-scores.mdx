# Ability Scores

Every character and creature in the game is defined by six **ability scores**. These scores measure natural talent and training, and they underpin nearly every roll you make.

## The Six Abilities

| Ability | Abbreviation | Governs |
|---|---|---|
| Strength | STR | Physical power, melee attacks, carrying capacity |
| Dexterity | DEX | Agility, ranged attacks, stealth, initiative |
| Constitution | CON | Endurance, hit points, resisting poison and disease |
| Intelligence | INT | Memory, reasoning, arcane magic |
| Wisdom | WIS | Perception, intuition, divine magic |
| Charisma | CHA | Personality, persuasion, performance |

## Score and Modifier

Each score ranges from 1 to 20 for most adventurers (though powerful creatures may exceed this). The **modifier** derived from the score is what actually gets added to rolls:

| Score | Modifier |
|---|---|
| 1 | −5 |
| 2–3 | −4 |
| 4–5 | −3 |
| 6–7 | −2 |
| 8–9 | −1 |
| 10–11 | +0 |
| 12–13 | +1 |
| 14–15 | +2 |
| 16–17 | +3 |
| 18–19 | +4 |
| 20 | +5 |

To calculate a modifier: **floor((score − 10) / 2)**.

## Generating Scores

At character creation, use one of the following methods as agreed upon by your group:

- **Standard Array:** Assign the values 15, 14, 13, 12, 10, 8 to your six abilities in any order.
- **Point Buy:** Start with all scores at 8 and spend 27 points. Scores 9–13 cost 1 point each; 14 costs 5 total; 15 costs 7 total.
- **Rolled:** Roll 4d6, drop the lowest die, and sum the remaining three. Repeat six times and assign freely.
