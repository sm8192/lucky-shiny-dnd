# Introduction

Welcome to the rulebook. This game is a collaborative storytelling experience in which one player takes on the role of the **Game Master (GM)** and the rest play as **adventurers** navigating a world shaped by imagination, strategy, and chance.

## What You Need to Play

- **2–6 players** (one of whom is the GM)
- A set of polyhedral dice (d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20)
- Character sheets
- Pencils and scratch paper

## The Core Loop

Each session is structured around a loop of three activities:

1. **The GM describes** the world, its inhabitants, and the current situation.
2. **Players declare** what their characters attempt to do.
3. **Dice resolve** uncertain outcomes, with results feeding back into the description.

Combat, exploration, and social encounters each elaborate on this loop with additional mechanics described in their respective chapters.

## The Golden Rule

When a rule conflicts with common sense or fun, the GM has final say. The rules exist to support the story, not replace it.
