# Exploration

Exploration covers everything adventurers do when they aren't in combat: travelling overland, delving into dungeons, negotiating with NPCs, and searching for clues.

## Travel Pace

When travelling overland, the group chooses a **pace** that determines how far they cover and what penalties apply.

| Pace | Distance per Hour | Distance per Day | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fast | 4 miles | 30 miles | −5 penalty to passive Perception |
| Normal | 3 miles | 24 miles | — |
| Slow | 2 miles | 18 miles | Able to use Stealth |

## Foraging and Survival

Characters can attempt to forage for food and water while travelling at a slow or normal pace. The GM sets a Survival check DC based on the terrain:

- **Plentiful (DC 10):** Forest, river delta
- **Moderate (DC 15):** Plains, hills
- **Scarce (DC 20):** Desert, arctic, high mountain

A successful check provides enough food and water for one person per point above the DC (minimum one).

## Resting

Rest is how characters recover resources spent during play.

### Short Rest
A short rest takes **1 hour**. During it, a character may spend any number of **Hit Dice** to recover HP. Roll each Hit Die and add your CON modifier; regain that many HP.

### Long Rest
A long rest takes **8 hours**, during which a character sleeps for at least 6. At the end of a long rest:
- Regain all lost HP
- Regain all spent Hit Dice (up to half your maximum, rounded up)
- Regain all daily class resources (spell slots, etc.)

A character can benefit from only one long rest per 24-hour period.
